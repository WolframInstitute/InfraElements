# Proposition IX.27

## Statement

If an even number is subtracted from an odd number, then the remainder is odd.

## Proof

Let the even number *BC* be subtracted from the odd number *AB.*

I say that the remainder *CA* is odd.

![java applet or image](propIX27.gif)

[VII.Def.7](../BookVII/DefVII.6.md)
Subtract the unit *AD,* therefore *DB* is even.

[IX.24](PropIX.24.md)
[VII.Def.7](../BookVII/DefVII.6.md)
But *BC* is also even, therefore the remainder *CD* is even. Therefore *CA* is odd.

Therefore, *if an even number is subtracted from an odd number, then the remainder is odd.*

Q.E.D.

## Guide

This is the last of four propositions that examine the result of subtracting even and odd numbers from each other.
