# Definition XI.28

## Statement

A dodecahedron is a solid figure contained by twelve equal, equilateral and equiangular pentagons.

## Guide

![tetrahedron](defXI25a.gif)
![cube](defXI25.gif)
![octahedron](defXI26.gif)

These are four of the five regular solids. The tetrahedron is not mentioned here since it is a certain triangular pyramid. It’s called simply the “pyramid” in Book XIII.

The regular tetrahedron is constructed in proposition [XIII.13](../BookXIII/PropXIII.13.md), the cube in [XIII.15](../BookXIII/PropXIII.15.md), the octahedron in [XIII.14](../BookXIII/PropXIII.14.md), the icosahedron in [XIII.16](../BookXIII/PropXIII.16.md), and the dodecahedron in [XIII.17](../BookXIII/PropXIII.17.md). These five are shown to be the only regular solids in proposition [XIII.18](../BookXIII/PropXIII.18.md).
