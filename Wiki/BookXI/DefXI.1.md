# Definition XI.1

## Statement

A solid is that which has length, breadth, and depth.

## Guide

*Note: in D. Joyce's edition the Guide is shared with Definition XI.1, Definition XI.2.*

The 28 definitions at the beginning of Book XI serve Books XII and XIII as well.

The first two definitions correspond to definitions [I.Def.2](../BookI/DefI.2.md) and [I.Def.3](../BookI/DefI.3.md) for a line and its ends, and definitions [I.Def.5](../BookI/DefI.5.md) and [I.Def.6](../BookI/DefI.6.md) for a surface and its edges.

spheres. [XI.Def.14](DefXI.14.md) through XI.Def.17, and propositions [XII.17](../BookXII/PropXII.17.md) and XII.18
