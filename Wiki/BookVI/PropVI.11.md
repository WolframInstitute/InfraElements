# Proposition VI.11

## Statement

To find a third proportional to two given straight lines.

## Proof

Let *AB* and *AC* be the two given straight lines, and let them be placed so as to contain any angle.

![java applet or image](propVI11.gif)

It is required to find a third proportional to *AB* and *AC.*

Produce them to the points *D* and *E,* and make *BD* equal to *AC.* Join *BC,* and draw *DE* through *D* parallel to it. ([I.3](../BookI/PropI.3.md), [I.31](../BookI/PropI.31.md))

Then since *BC* is parallel to a side *DE* of the triangle *ADE,* therefore, proportionally, *AB* is to *BD* as *AC* is to *CE.* ([VI.2](PropVI.2.md))

But *BD* equals *AC,* therefore *AB* is to *AC* as *AC* is to *CE.* ([V.7](../BookV/PropV.7.md))

Therefore a third proportional *CE* has been found to two given straight lines *AB* and *AC.*

Q.E.F.

## Guide

If a and b are two magnitudes, then their third proportional is a magnitude c such that a : b = b : c. The third proportional is needed whenever a duplicate ratio is needed when the ratio itself is known. The duplicate ratio for a : b is a : c.

Use of this proposition

This construction is used in propositions [VI.19](PropVI.19.md),
[VI.22](PropVI.22.md), and a few propositions in Book X.
