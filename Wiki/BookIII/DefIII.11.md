# Definition III.11

## Statement

Similar segments of circles are those which admit equal angles, or in which the angles equal one another.

## Guide

![java applet or image](defIII11.gif)

Since the segments *BGC* and *EHF* have in them equal angles *BGC* and *EHF,* this definition declares them to be similar segments.

This is hardly a proper definition considering that proposition [III.21](PropIII.21.md) has yet to be proved in which it is shown that all the angles in one segment are equal.

Besides III.21, similar segments are mentioned in [III.24](PropIII.24.md) and [III.26](PropIII.26.md).
