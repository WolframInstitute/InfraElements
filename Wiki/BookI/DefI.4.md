# Definition I.4

## Statement

A *straight line* is a line which lies evenly with the points on itself.

## Guide

This statement indicates, at least, that the term “straight line” refers to a kind of line. It is hard to tell what else it means, if anything. Various commentators have interpreted in a variety of ways. The definition of plane surface in [I.Def.7](DefI.7.md) uses a similar language that is equally opaque.

There are a some postulates that come a little later in Book I and give meaning to straight lines. [I.Post.1](PostI.1.md) says that a straight line can be drawn between any two points, [I.Post.2](PostI.2.md) says that a straight line can be extended, and the remaining postulates use the concept of straight line in one way or another.
